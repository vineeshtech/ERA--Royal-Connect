Royal Connect is the alumni network of Royal College of Engineering & Technology (RCET), where graduates, their families and their businesses help each other. Ask for a quotation, request help or advice, share a job opening or post your CV, all within a community you already trust.

What you can do

Three ways RCET alumni help each other

Quotations & help requests

Need a supplier, a contractor or simply good advice? Post it once and fellow alumni respond with a quotation or an offer to help.

  • Ask for quotations from alumni businesses
  • Ask for help, advice or a contact
  • Mentor current RCET students

Alumni business directory

Every business run by an RCET graduate or their family, searchable by service, batch and department.

  • Buy from alumni first
  • Family-run businesses linked to the alumnus
  • Request a quote from any listing

Jobs & referrals

Is your company hiring? Post the vacancy for RCET graduates. Looking for a move? Upload your CV so alumni can refer you.

  • Post jobs from your company
  • Apply with your CV in one step
  • Juniors get referred by seniors
How it works

From registration to helping a batchmate

1

Register

Sign up with your RCET batch year and department, or as a family member of an alumnus.

2

Get verified

The alumni committee confirms your batch, so everyone on the network is a genuine RCETian or family.

3

Post or respond

Share a request, send a quotation, offer help, post a job or upload your CV.

4

Connect directly

Talk to the alumnus, agree the details and keep the opportunity within the RCET family.

Alumni families welcome

Your family's business belongs here too

Many RCET graduates run businesses with parents, spouses and siblings. Family members can register and link to the alumnus, so they can post requests, send quotations and hire on behalf of the family business.
